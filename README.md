For the scene i broke the game down into what i saw was important. moving, passing and slam dunks. so i made the player be able to pass the ball to a non moving ally and slam dunk when the hit the net with the ball in their hand. All the assets are primitives but it all works pretty good for a basic prototype.

For my first shader i made a reflection shader for the floor. It works by taking the cubemap, and using the viewing angle to get the reflection and convert that world space into the object space. That way it looks like its reflecting from whatever way your looking. I also messed with some of the variables for the shader to make the reflection less like a mirror and more like a basketball court, where the texture is the focus but the reflection is complementing it.
